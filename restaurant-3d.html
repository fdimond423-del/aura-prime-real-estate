From Dawn Soil to Crystal Plate

The Four-Stage Culinary Discipline

Every evening begins before sunrise. Follow our disciplined provenance chain from single-estate harvests to tableside presentation.

01

Dawn Micro-Harvest

Heirloom botanicals, micro-herbs, and edible blossoms are clipped at peak morning turgor from our private conservatory.

02

Salt-Cave Maturation

Prime cuts undergo controlled enzymatic tenderization for 45 days in bespoke Himalayan pink-salt chambers.

03

Thermal Precision

Courses are plated onto 58°C carved Icelandic volcanic basalt stones to protect aromatic volatile compounds.

04

Sommelier Choreography

Each plate is synchronized to a half-glass pour from our Grand Cru vault, balancing tannin, acidity, and umami.
