Questions

Frequently asked

How long does it take to build my website?
Most websites are ready within 2 working days of receiving all your content. Rush delivery in 24 hours is available on WhatsApp — just ask.
Can I see it on mobile before it goes live?
Yes. We send you a private preview link before the final version. You can request edits within 3 days of receiving it at no extra cost.
Will my guests need to download an app?
No app needed. The website opens directly in any browser — Chrome, Safari, or any browser. Guests just tap the link you share on WhatsApp or Instagram.
What happens after one year of hosting?
We will notify you before the year ends. Renewal is ₹999 for another year. Your website and all memories remain safe — we will never delete anything without your permission.
Can I use a template for a Muslim, Christian or Punjabi wedding?
Yes! We have templates for different traditions — Noor for Muslim weddings, Capella for Christian, Phulkari for Punjabi and more. Message us on WhatsApp and we will recommend the right one for your ceremony.
