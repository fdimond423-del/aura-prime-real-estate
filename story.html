✦ THE SOVEREIGN ROASTERY ✦

Rooted in Soil, Honored in the Cup.

Founded in 2024 by roaster Kenjiro Nakamura and master baker Claire Dubois, KŌHĪ Atelier was born from one quiet conviction: that the world does not need more fast coffee, it needs more sacred pauses.

01.

High-Altitude Terroir & Direct Harvest

We never buy commodity coffee through broker auctions. We travel directly to small family-owned farms at elevations above 1,800 meters in Ethiopia, Panama, and Colombia. Cooler mountain nights force coffee cherries to ripen slowly, concentrating organic sugars and floral aromatics.

02.

Cast-Iron Micro-Batch Roasting

Dark roasting kills the identity of the coffee. At KŌHĪ, we roast strictly in 5kg batches on custom cast-iron drums with infrared burner curves. We stop the roast right after first crack to preserve the natural jasmine, bergamot, and peach notes innate to the terroir.

03.

Mineral Architecture of Water

A cup of coffee is 98.5% water. We re-mineralize pure reverse-osmosis water with precise ratios of magnesium (to extract bright floral acids) and calcium (to carry silky body). Poured at an exact 93.5°C using swan-neck kettles with steady spiral turbulence.

04.

72-Hour Wild Levain Sourdough

Our bakery dough undergoes cold fermentation over three days using our 8-year-old sourdough starter named ‘Fuji’. Layered with 84% butterfat Normandy cultured butter, our croissants emerge with 32 distinct honeycombed layers that shatter delicately with every bite.
