# Designing Animated Websites

## Philosophy
- **Clean & Uncrowded:** Never build overcrowded, noisy pages. Prioritize white-space, breathing room, deliberate typography, and high-editorial restraint.
- **Micro-Interactions & Scroll-Driven Motion:** Use GSAP ScrollTrigger, smooth scrubbing, text split reveals, magnetic buttons, and organic material physics.
- **Authentic Materiality:** Warm natural tones, rich organic textures, crisp serif & sans pairings.
- **Auto-Playing Visuals:** Hero visuals with ambient motion (steaming cup, roasting beans, video loops) playing automatically without click friction.
