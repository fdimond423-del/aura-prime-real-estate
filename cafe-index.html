STEP 01 / HIGH-ELEVATION TERROIR

Volcanic Mountain Soils & Wild Shade

Grown under dense indigenous canopies in Ethiopia's Bench Maji region. Slow ripening at 1,950 meters concentrates organic acids and creates dense, sugars-rich cherries hand-harvested at peak brix.

STEP 02 / UNIMODAL PARTICLE ARCHITECTURE

Cast-Iron Micro Mill Precision

Milled at exactly 800 microns using custom-hardened cast iron conical burrs. The unimodal particle distribution eliminates micro-fines, guaranteeing clean, crystalline floral notes without astringency.

STEP 03 / THE 93.5°C BLOOM DANCE

45-Second Degassing & Gentle Saturation

A 60g pour of hot mineralized water unlocks trapped carbon dioxide. The coffee bed swells into a fragrant dome, releasing honeysuckle and bergamot aromas into the atelier air.

STEP 04 / THE FINAL EXTRACTION

Crystalline Clarity in Handcrafted Ceramic

Filtered through unbleached abaca paper fibers into hand-thrown Karatsu stoneware cups. Silky body, vibrant lingering stone fruit sweetness, and an immaculate tea-like finish.
