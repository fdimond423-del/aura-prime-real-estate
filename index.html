✦ HOW OUR JOURNEY BEGAN ✦

Our Story

From serendipitous coffee conversations to a lifetime of adventures, hand in hand.

Olivia & Adrian · Sunset in Como
“What began as simple conversations soon turned into a love story we never saw coming.”

We were two people walking different paths, who found in each other a sense of ease, kindness, and belonging. What started with an impromptu conversation on a rainy autumn evening in Soho quickly became our favorite daily chapter.

Over the years, we discovered our shared passion for quiet morning coffees, spontaneous travels, and cooking dinners filled with laughter. On a sun-drenched terrace overlooking Lake Como, Adrian asked, and through happy tears, Olivia whispered yes.
